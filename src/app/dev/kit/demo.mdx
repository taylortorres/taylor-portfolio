<Section id="overview" title="Overview">
**Try:** scroll and watch the sidebar follow along (Overview is active at the very top). Click a section to smooth-scroll there. Tab through the sidebar with the keyboard. After about one screen, BACK TO TOP fades in at the bottom of the sidebar (bottom-right on phones).

**Page transition:** reload this page, or go [home](/) and come back with the browser or a link. The new page sharpens from a soft blur as it fades in. On client navigations the old page also fades out.

Phones and tablets (below 1024px) get a sticky bar instead of the sidebar. Tap the current section to open the list.
</Section>
